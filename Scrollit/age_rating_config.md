# App Store Age Rating Configuration

## Required Settings in App Store Connect

Navigate to: **App Store Connect > Your App > App Information > Age Rating > Manage**

### Sexual Content or Nudity
- **Select: Frequent**
- Reason: App displays Reddit content which may include NSFW (Not Safe For Work) posts tagged as over_18. These posts are filtered for non-logged-in users and require login to view.

### Mature/Suggestive Themes
- **Select: Frequent**
- Reason: Reddit content may contain mature themes.

### Profanity or Crude Humor
- **Select: Frequent**
- Reason: User-generated content from Reddit may contain profanity.

### Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use or References
- **Select: None** (unless your app specifically targets these topics)

### Simulated Gambling
- **Select: None**

### Realistic Violence
- **Select: None**

### Horror/Fear Themes
- **Select: None**

### Medical/Treatment Information
- **Select: None**

### Sexual Content or Nudity (additional)
- **Select: Frequent** (as required by Apple review)

## Final Age Rating
The app will likely receive a **17+** rating due to Frequent Sexual Content/Nudity selection.

## Important Notes
1. This rating is required because the app displays user-generated content from Reddit
2. NSFW content is properly filtered for non-logged-in users
3. All NSFW content is visibly tagged with "NSFW" labels
4. Users can report objectionable content and block abusive users
5. The developer commits to acting on reports within 24 hours
