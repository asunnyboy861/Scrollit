## Description
Scrollit is a minimalist community browser designed for people who want a fast, clean, and stable way to browse their favorite communities on iPhone and iPad.

Tired of ads, crashes, and sluggish video players in other apps? Scrollit gives you a pure, distraction-free browsing experience with native iOS performance.

KEY FEATURES:

- Ad-Free Browsing: No promoted posts, no banner ads, no sponsored content. Just the posts you actually want to see.

- Lightning Fast: Built natively for iOS with smooth infinite scrolling and instant content loading. No more waiting for pages to render.

- Seamless Media Player: Watch videos and GIFs without stuttering. Full-screen playback with speed control, gesture-based navigation, and smooth transitions.

- Smart Content Filters: Block keywords and content from specific communities. Create custom filter rules to curate your feed exactly how you want it.

- Full Account Experience: Sign in with your account to vote, comment, post, and save. Access your subscribed communities and inbox.

- Multiple Sort Options: Browse by Hot, New, Top, or Rising. Switch sorting on the fly to find exactly what you are looking for.

- Threaded Comments: Read and participate in comment threads with collapsible replies, voting, and inline replies.

- Image Viewer: Pinch-to-zoom, double-tap to zoom, and swipe to dismiss. View images in full resolution without leaving the app.

- Dark Mode: Full support for iOS dark mode. Easy on the eyes for late-night browsing sessions.

- iPad Optimized: Designed to take full advantage of the larger screen with a comfortable reading layout.

- Privacy First: We do not track your browsing habits or sell your data. Your activity stays private.
